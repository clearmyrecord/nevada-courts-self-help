Record relief: a proposed RecordPathAI integration

See how a separate, opt-in service could help people understand possible criminal-record sealing or expungement eligibility and prepare filing materials. This section demonstrates a potential future integration—not a Nevada Judiciary service, endorsement, approved filing process, or representation that Nevada eligibility rules have been validated.

1. Explore eligibility

Learn about eligibility screening. Actual eligibility depends on the state, case disposition, offense and applicable law.

2. Review case details

RecordPathAI's separate workflow may request case information to evaluate possible relief. This court-site prototype does not collect or transfer personal or criminal-record data.

3. Prepare next steps

Where supported, users can review packet preparation options and applicable costs. No packet is filed or purchased through this demonstration.
